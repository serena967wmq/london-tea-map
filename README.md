# London tea places map

Interactive map of tea places in London (bubble tea, pure tea and teahouses, tea-leaf shops), by estimated food-hygiene registration year. Drag the time bar; filter by type and brand origin.

Data: Food Standards Agency food hygiene rating open data (19 Nov 2026) and other public sources listed on the map. Registration year is not the opening date; closed shops drop out of the register. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
